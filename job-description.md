## Software Engineer | New York City Area | Analyst

New York, NY, United States

## Job Description

**About Goldman Sachs**

The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc. is a leading global investment banking, securities, and investment management firm that provides a wide range of financial services to a substantial and diversified client base that includes corporations, financial institutions, governments, and individuals. Founded in 1869, the firm is headquartered in New York and maintains offices in all major financial centers around the world.

**About Engineering**

At Goldman Sachs, our Engineers don’t just make things—we make things possible. We change the world by connecting people and capital with ideas. You will join a team of innovators solving the most challenging and pressing engineering problems for our clients. Our engineering teams build massively scalable software and systems, architect low-latency infrastructure solutions, proactively guard against cyber threats, and leverage machine learning alongside financial engineering to continuously turn data into action. Create new businesses, transform finance, and explore a world of opportunity at the speed of markets.

**About the Role**

As a Software Engineering Analyst, you will apply your technical expertise to build the next generation of financial systems. You will work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment, utilizing modern open-source software, cloud solutions, and high-performance programming languages to solve complex business challenges.

Analysts are integrated into one of two primary engineering tracks:

- **Embedded Engineering:** Work directly with business partners in trading, sales, asset management, and banking to build and automate solutions that provide a competitive edge.
- **Foundation Engineering:** Develop and maintain the firm’s foundational platforms, including data engineering, machine learning infrastructure, developer tooling, and global networking.

**Key Responsibilities**

- **System Development:** Manage the end-to-end software development lifecycle (SDLC) within an Agile framework, including requirements gathering, design, implementation, and maintenance.
- **Technical Innovation:** Design and deploy scalable, low-latency applications and technical solutions that enhance global operations.
- **Collaboration:** Partner with cross-functional teams to understand business workflows and translate them into robust technical specifications.
- **Optimization:** Continuously improve existing systems to increase efficiency, reliability, and functionality.
- **Problem Solving:** Resolve complex technical issues and provide data-driven insights to support firm-wide decision-making.

**Basic Qualifications**

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field.
- 1–2 years of prior work experience in a relevant software engineering or technical analyst role.
- Strong proficiency in at least one core programming language (e.g., Java, Python, C++, or C#).
- Solid understanding of Data Structures, Algorithms, and Software Design patterns.
- Experience with SQL and relational database management.
- Exceptional attention to detail and a high degree of organizational skill.
- Ability to manage multiple projects with competing deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
- Integrity, ethical standards, and sound judgment when handling confidential information.

**Preferred Qualifications**

- Experience with cloud-native technologies (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Familiarity with modern software engineering practices, including version control (Git), CI/CD pipelines, and automated testing.
- Knowledge of distributed systems and microservices architecture.
- Strong communication skills with the ability to present technical concepts to non-technical business partners.
- Interest in financial markets and the application of technology within the financial services industry.
